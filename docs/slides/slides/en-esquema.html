What a semantic layer is

A business dictionary between the sources and whoever asks

Rastro · Oil and gas in Argentina

3

What to say (45 seconds): on the left, the databases as they come; each one stores reality its own way. In the middle, Rastro: a dictionary in the business's words (company, concession, well), how each database translates into those words, how the same company is linked across databases (each link with its evidence) and rules that check the data. The data is not copied: each question is translated to the databases on the fly. On the right, who uses it: people, AI assistants, models and other tools, because everything is in open standards. If asked how it compares to a data warehouse: it does not replace the databases or copy the data; it puts the definitions and the links in one place.
